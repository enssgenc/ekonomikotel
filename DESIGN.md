# Ekonomikotel visual direction

## Direction
Bright Turkish travel retail, selected explicitly by the user through ETS Tur and Ekonomiktatilim references. This replaces a maintenance page. The catalogue and photography provide richness; controls stay familiar and quick. No fabricated offers or borrowed competitor branding.

## System
- Light theme across the main travel site. White surfaces, cool pale blue canvas, dark blue ink, one cobalt action color.
- Self-hosted Hanken Grotesk for headings and DM Sans for UI/body, including Turkish Latin Extended glyphs.
- Maximum content width 1240px. Generous gutters; search is visible in the first viewport. Main desktop navigation stays one line.
- Radius: cards 16px, fields/buttons 10px, compact filter pills fully rounded. A functional product card uses one border; the overlapping search surface uses a shadow.
- Phosphor icons only in the new travel interface, 1.5-like regular weight. Preserve original health site's existing art and identity.
- Design Taste dials: DESIGN_VARIANCE 4, MOTION_INTENSITY 3, VISUAL_DENSITY 5. A repeat-use commerce search flow needs predictable placement and quick interactions.
- Emil motion: 120ms press feedback, 180ms origin-aware guest popover, no scroll-jacking, no autoplay carousel, reduced-motion respected.

## Surface contract
Home: one destination photograph, left-aligned two-line headline, a white search surface with hotel/tour tabs, then photo-led hotel selection, a two-package travel comparison, district exploration, and an integrated health journey entry. The signature interaction is the compact guest selector, whose summary always reflects the actual request.
Hotel catalogue: search context, real result count, meaningful region/facility/name filters, sorting and load-more. All hotels use local photographs. No invented price sorting.
Details: gallery, unabridged source description, source amenities, location link, and dated offer request. Gallery is keyboard accessible.

## Refinement — 25 September 2026
The follow-up design uses the existing warm Cappadocia terrace photograph in an inset desktop hero, a shorter destination-led headline, and clearer search mode selection. Featured hotels have working region tabs, larger photography, legible amenity text and real gallery counts. Mobile featured cards scroll horizontally with a visible next card and a scroll hint. Tour packages sit on a warm neutral surface; destination cards use image overlays for stronger geographic discovery. Supplier descriptions and the separate health content are preserved.
